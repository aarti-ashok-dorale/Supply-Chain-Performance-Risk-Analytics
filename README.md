# Supply Chain Performance & Risk Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

An End-to-End Data Analyst project focused on analyzing supply chain performance, supplier reliability, operational risks, delivery delays, shipping costs, and overall supply chain efficiency.

I completed this project during my Data Analytics practice journey in **June 2026**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Supply chain operations are affected by multiple factors such as supplier reliability, inventory levels, lead time, shipping costs, equipment availability, weather conditions, route risk, customs clearance, and delivery delays.

The objective of this project was to identify major risk areas, understand operational performance, and provide data-driven recommendations to support better supply chain decisions.

## 💡 Approach

The project was completed using an end-to-end analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Python Data Cleaning → EDA → SQL Business Analysis → Power BI & DAX → Insights → Recommendations**

### 🐍 Python & Jupyter Notebook
Used Python for data cleaning, preprocessing, data validation, handling data quality issues, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

### 🗄️ SQL
Used SQL to answer business questions related to supplier performance, shipping costs, lead time, risk classification, delivery delays, inventory, fulfillment, and supplier countries.

### 📊 Power BI & DAX
Created an interactive **2-page dashboard** to monitor supply chain performance and risk.

**Page 1 – Supply Chain Performance**
- Product & Supplier KPIs
- Product Count by Risk Class
- Average Lead Time by Risk Class
- Shipping Cost by Supplier Country
- Delay Probability by Risk Class
- Average Route Risk by Supplier Country

**Page 2 – Risk Analysis & Final Insights**
- Equipment Availability Distribution
- Weather Severity Distribution
- Customs Clearance vs Cargo Condition
- Delivery Deviation Analysis
- Key Insights & Recommendations

## 🔍 Key Insights

- High-risk products dominate the supply chain.
- Major delivery delays are significantly higher than on-time and minor delays.
- Lead time is slightly higher for high-risk products.
- Shipping costs vary considerably across supplier countries.
- Route risk differs across supplier locations.
- Low equipment availability may contribute to operational disruptions.
- Weather severity remains an important operational factor.

## 🎯 Recommendations

- Prioritize high-risk products and suppliers for continuous monitoring.
- Improve supplier and route risk management.
- Optimize high-cost supplier routes.
- Maintain sufficient inventory buffers for high-risk products.
- Improve equipment availability.
- Monitor lead time and delay probability regularly.
- Focus on reducing major delivery delays through proactive planning.
